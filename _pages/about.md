## About Me

Like many in this field, I grew up as a gamer. I spent too many hours playing and wondering what it would be like to see my name in the credits one day. That dream stuck with me - and eventually, it came true.

I've dedicated both my undergraduate and postgraduate studies to games technology, and since then, I've worked on a range of projects using Unreal Engine, Unity, and backend systems. I'm someone who genuinely enjoys digging into complex systems, solving tricky problems, and figuring out how everything works under the hood.

I don't claim to be the fastest learner in the room, but I've always believed that consistency beats speed. I just keep showing up, keep learning, and keep building - and that's made all the difference in my journy so far.
