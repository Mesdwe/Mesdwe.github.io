# About Me

<p class="lede">Games programmer with Unreal Engine, Unity, and backend engineering experience.</p>

Like many in this field, I grew up as a gamer. I spent too many hours playing and wondering what it would be like to see my name in the credits one day. That dream stuck with me - and eventually, it came true.

I dedicated both my undergraduate and postgraduate studies to games technology, and since then, I have worked on a range of projects using Unreal Engine, Unity, and backend systems. I genuinely enjoy digging into complex systems, solving tricky problems, and figuring out how everything works under the hood.

I do not claim to be the fastest learner in the room, but I have always believed that consistency beats speed. I keep showing up, keep learning, and keep building - and that has made all the difference in my journey so far.
